02 — Case studies

Problem → system → result

Sova Health · Quick commerce · 8 months

Scaling a health brand on 10‑minute delivery apps

Problem
Quick-commerce was a small channel at roughly ₹50 lakh in revenue, spread across three platforms with different replenishment rules.
What I did
Ran operations across Blinkit, Zepto and Swiggy Instamart, keeping stock flowing to dark stores alongside Shopify, Amazon and Flipkart operations.
Result
₹4.3 crore in 8 months — about 8.6× growth.

Sova Health · Automation

Turning a two-day order queue into ten minutes

Problem
Orders took around 48 hours to process before they could move to dispatch.
What I did
Built an automation for the order-processing flow on the Unicommerce dispatch system.
Result
Turnaround cut to ~10 minutes — a 99.7% reduction.

GLEUHR Wellness · Inventory & dispatch · Now

A dispatch engine for domestic and international orders

Problem
Clinic and complementary product lines, three order channels, and shipments both inside India and abroad.
What I do
Own the inventory lifecycle, reorder points for fast movers, Shiprocket domestic and DHL international with customs paperwork, plus daily/weekly/monthly MIS. I lead a 4‑person team.
Result
Stockouts eliminated on fast-moving SKUs within budget, 24–48h dispatch SLA, fewer expiry write-offs.
